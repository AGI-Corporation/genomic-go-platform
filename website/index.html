Flagship dataset

A CRBN-targeted library, fully characterized

Every one of the 425 AGI compounds is designed on an imidazo[4,5-c]pyridine scaffold and modeled for how it modulates the CRBN E3 ubiquitin ligase to induce apoptosis in cancer cells. Each entry carries its SMILES structure, substituent, molecular weight, LogP, binding free energy (ΔG), predicted IC50, and a drug-likeness goodness score.

  • Searchable, filterable, sortable by potency and drug-likeness
  • 2D structure rendering for every compound
  • Semantic search across mechanism and target
  • Secured, access-controlled — data stays protected
